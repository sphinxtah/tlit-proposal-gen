    Tax
    Tax is not shown in the proposal or the SOW; fees are quoted before tax and tax is invoiced separately. Tax depends on the service, software licensing, and transaction location. Scottsdale lists 8.00% for most taxable activities; that is not automatically the correct rate for every client. See the tax research notes before applying a rate.
